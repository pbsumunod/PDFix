10. `10-native-result.png` (1.1.0): headline changed from “Make room for what matters.” to “Make room for new ideas.” by genuinely replacing the original PDF text, preserving its original font, color, and background.

The native edit was exported through Android Files to `../native-after.pdf`. Independent PDF checks confirmed the old headline words were removed, the new words were present, font programs were unchanged, and the other text, second-page content stream, and three form values were preserved. This demonstrates a supported text-run replacement, not paragraph reflow or universal PDF text editing.
